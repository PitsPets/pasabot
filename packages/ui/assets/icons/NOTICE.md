# Icon tiles: sources and licences

The SVGs in this folder are the marks `BrandTile` draws. `manifest.json` lists, for each one, its id, source,
licence and brand-guideline link. The full list, the rules and how to add a brand: `docs/BRANDS.md`.

- **Brand marks** (`kind: "brand"`): the real marks from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) 16.36.0,
  CC0-1.0, generated by `packages/ui/scripts/gen-brands.ts` into `packages/ui/src/icons/brand-data.gen.ts` and the
  SVGs here. CC0 grants no trademark rights: brand icons from Simple Icons (CC0). Trademarks belong to their owners;
  used only to name the service, always next to its name.
- **Git**: "Git Logo by Jason Long", licensed under
  [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) (via Simple Icons 16.36.0).
- **Lettermarks** (`kind: "letter"`, no SVG): brands Simple Icons does not ship (for example OpenAI/Codex, Grok,
  Slack, Outlook, Amazon, Lazada). The tile shows the first letter of the name; no imitation of the mark is drawn.
- **Own glyphs** (`kind: "own"`): drawn for PasaBot in its line style (24 px grid, stroke 1.75, round caps),
  Apache-2.0 like the rest of PasaBot.
