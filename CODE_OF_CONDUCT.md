# Code of conduct

PasaBot is built by people with different languages, backgrounds and levels of experience. We want every one of
them to feel welcome here.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and with people writing in their second language.
- Disagree about ideas, not about people. Assume good intent and ask before you judge.
- Give credit, accept feedback, and own your mistakes.
- Keep conversations on topic and useful.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions or ignoring a request to stop.

## Scope

This applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any chat about the project,
and when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Tell the maintainer (PitsPets) privately. If you have no private way to reach them, open an issue titled
"Conduct report: please contact me", with no details, and the maintainer will reach out. Reports are handled
confidentially.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, and warn, suspend or ban anyone who breaks this code, in
proportion to what happened.
